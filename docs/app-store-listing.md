## Description — 4000 char limit

recipHub is the calm home for every recipe you love — and the plan that gets it on the table.

Save a recipe from anywhere: paste a link, drop in a screenshot or a photo of a cookbook page, or just type it out. recipHub pulls it into a clean, consistent card — no ads, no ten-paragraph life story, just the recipe.

SAVE ANY RECIPE
• Import from any link or food blog
• Snap a photo of a handwritten card or cookbook page
• Paste text or add it by hand
• Organize into cookbooks and favorites

PLAN YOUR WEEK
• Drop recipes onto any day
• See the whole week at a glance
• Rearrange with a tap

ONE GROCERY LIST, DONE FOR YOU
• Turn a week's plan into a single shopping list
• Snap a photo of a grocery note to add items instantly
• Check items off as you shop; duplicates merge automatically
• Share the list with anyone

COOK WITHOUT THE MESS
• Hands-free cooking mode with step timers and read-aloud
• A screen that stays awake while you cook
• Adapt any recipe with the AI assistant — dairy-free, gluten-free, lower-calorie

FOR THE WHOLE HOUSE
• One live grocery list the whole family shares, synced in real time

KNOW WHAT YOU'RE EATING
• Daily macro and calorie targets
• Weekly insights on balance and variety
