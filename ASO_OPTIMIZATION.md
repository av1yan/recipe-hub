## Recommended Optimizations

### 1. **Title Optimization**
**Current Consideration:** The title "recipHub" is short but doesn't include high-value keywords.

**Recommended Options:**
- **Option A (Conservative):** `recipHub: Save & Plan Meals` 
  - Adds "Save" and "Plan" keywords while keeping brand identity
  - Length: 27 characters (under the 30-char limit)
  
- **Option B (Aggressive):** `Recipe Meal Planner - recipHub`
  - Leads with keywords "Recipe" and "Meal Planner"
  - Targets "meal plan" search term
  - Length: 31 characters (slightly over, may need adjustment)

**Recommendation:** **Option A** - maintains brand identity while adding relevant keywords.
